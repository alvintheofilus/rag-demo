# When the dog does not spin

The most common cause is a lure that moves too fast or too high. A treat held above the dog's
head makes it sit or jump instead of turning. Keep the treat at nose height and move it in a
circle the dog can physically follow.

The second most common cause is a low-value reward. Kibble is not worth working for in a room
where a squirrel was outside five minutes ago. Try a piece of cooked chicken and see whether
the dog offers the turn without hesitation.

Distraction is the third cause. A dog that keeps checking the door is not refusing the trick,
it is unable to focus. Move to a smaller room, close the door, and remove other animals from
the space before trying again.

Never push the dog's body around to force the turn. Physical pressure makes the trick feel
like something done to the dog rather than something it offers, and it slows learning down.
If ten repetitions in a row fail, end the session and try again tomorrow.
