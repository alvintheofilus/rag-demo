# Teaching a dog to spin

Spin is a trick where the dog turns a full circle in place. It is easy to teach because the
dog already knows how to follow a treat with its nose.

Start in a quiet room with no other dogs and a floor that does not slip. Hold a small treat
between your thumb and finger, put it right at the dog's nose, and move your hand in a slow
circle to the dog's left. The nose follows the treat, the body follows the nose. Most dogs
complete a circle on the third or fourth attempt.

Mark the moment the dog finishes the circle with a click or a quiet "yes", then give the
treat. The marker is what tells the dog which part of the movement earned the food.

Keep each session between two and three minutes. Three short sessions a day beats one long
one, because a tired or full dog stops offering behaviour. Stop while the dog is still
keen, not after it walks away.
