# Fading the treat and adding a cue

Once the dog completes the circle reliably, take the lure away. Move your hand in the same
circle but empty, and reward from your other hand after the dog finishes. Do this for three
or four repetitions before adding a spoken cue.

Add the cue word "spin" just before the hand movement, not after. The order is: say the word
once, pause half a second, then make the hand signal. Repeating the word while the dog is
already turning teaches it that the word means nothing.

After roughly twenty successful repetitions the hand signal can shrink to a small circular
flick at waist height, and later to nothing at all. Dogs read position and posture, so keep
your body still when you test whether the word alone works.

Reward every repetition at first, then every second one, then randomly. Intermittent rewards
make the behaviour more resistant to extinction than paying every single time.
